BACKGROUND

Northstar entered into a twelve-month equipment supply and maintenance agreement with Meridian on January 15, 2026. Meridian agreed to deliver and install forty ergonomic workstations and provide quarterly maintenance services. The total contract price was INR 2,400,000, payable in four equal installments.

The agreement required all forty workstations to be delivered by February 15, 2026. Northstar paid the first installment of INR 600,000 on January 20, 2026. Meridian delivered twenty-eight workstations on February 18, 2026 and delivered the remaining twelve on March 12, 2026.

Northstar reports that eight workstations developed hydraulic faults during April 2026. Northstar sent written notices to Meridian on April 22 and May 6. Meridian repaired three workstations but did not inspect the remaining five.

CONTRACT TERMS

Clause 4.2 requires Meridian to repair or replace defective equipment within ten business days after receiving written notice.

Clause 7.1 permits Northstar to withhold a disputed installment after giving Meridian seven calendar days written notice describing the dispute.

Clause 9.3 requires the parties to attend a good-faith settlement meeting before commencing arbitration.

Clause 11.2 requires any arbitration demand to be filed within one year after the disputed breach became known to the claimant.

Clause 13.1 states that notices must be sent by email and registered post to the addresses listed in the agreement.

CURRENT DISPUTE

Meridian issued an invoice for the third installment of INR 600,000 on August 28, 2026. The invoice states that payment is due on October 5, 2026.

Northstar disputes the invoice because five workstations remain defective and because delivery was completed twenty-five days late. On September 26, 2026, Northstar emailed Meridian stating that it intended to withhold the third installment. The current file does not contain evidence that the notice was also sent by registered post.

Meridian responded on September 28, 2026. It denied responsibility for the remaining faults, alleged misuse by Northstar employees, and demanded full payment by October 5, 2026. Meridian stated that it may suspend all maintenance services if payment is not received.
